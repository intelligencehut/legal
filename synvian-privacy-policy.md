## 3. Information we collect

### 3.1 Information you give us

- **Account details:** name, work email address, job title, the clinic or organisation you
  belong to, and your role in Synvian.
- **Sign-in credentials:** if you use a password, we store it only as a one-way hash. If you
  sign in with Google, see Section 5.
- **Support and sales communications:** messages you send us, demo requests, and the details
  you enter on contact forms.

### 3.2 Information collected automatically

- **Usage and device data:** the pages and features you use, timestamps, browser type, operating
  system, screen size, language setting, and approximate location derived from your IP address.
- **Security and audit logs:** sign-in events, IP addresses, session identifiers, and a record of
  the actions taken in the application. Audit logs are a legal and security requirement for a
  healthcare system and cannot be opted out of.
- **Application diagnostics:** error reports and performance measurements. These are designed
  not to contain patient information.

### 3.3 Information from other sources

- **Your organisation:** the clinic administrator who invites you to Synvian provides your name,
  email address and role.
- **Google:** the profile information described in Section 5, only if you choose to sign in with
  Google.

## 4. Patient health information

Clinics use Synvian to store and manage patient records. This information is **Customer Data**.

- The clinic is responsible for collecting patient consent and for telling patients how their
  information is used.
- We process Customer Data only to provide the service to the clinic, to keep it secure, and as
  the law requires. We do not use patient information for advertising, for product marketing, or
  to train general-purpose machine-learning models.
- Where a clinic is a covered entity or business associate under the United States Health
  Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), we sign a Business Associate Agreement
  before any protected health information is stored in Synvian.
- When a clinic's subscription ends, Customer Data is returned or deleted as described in the
  Customer Agreement.

Nothing in this policy gives Intelligence Hut any ownership of patient records.

## 5. Signing in with Google

Synvian offers "Sign in with Google" as a convenience. If you use it, Google shares the following
with us from your Google account:

- your name
- your email address
- your profile picture
- a unique Google account identifier

We use this information only to create and secure your Synvian account, to show your name and
picture inside the application, and to send you service messages. We do not request access to
your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Contacts or any other Google data unless a specific feature
asks for it, and that feature will ask for your separate permission first.

**Limited Use disclosure.** Synvian's use and transfer to any other application of information
received from Google APIs will adhere to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

- we use Google user data only to provide and improve user-facing features of Synvian;
- we do not sell Google user data;
- we do not use Google user data for advertising;
- no person at Intelligence Hut reads Google user data unless you have given consent, it is
  needed for security or to comply with law, or it has been aggregated and anonymised.

You can withdraw Synvian's access at any time at
[myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Your Synvian
account continues to exist and you can sign in with another method.

## 8. Cookies and similar technologies

- **Essential cookies** keep you signed in and protect against request forgery. The
  application does not work without them.
- **Preference cookies** remember your language and display choices.
- **Analytics** on the public website measure page visits in aggregate. We do not run analytics
  or advertising trackers inside the signed-in application, and no patient information is ever
  sent to an analytics provider.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies prevents
sign-in.

## 9. Security

Synvian is built for health information. Among other measures:

- all data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and especially sensitive fields are encrypted
  separately at the application layer;
- every clinic's data is isolated from every other clinic's at the database level;
- access is controlled by role, and every read or change of a patient record is written to an
  audit log;
- staff access to production systems is limited, logged and reviewed.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your information we will
notify the affected clinic, and the authorities where required, without undue delay.
